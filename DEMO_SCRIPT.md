# Northstar Sign - Demo Video Script

**Objective:** Demonstrate the full lifecycle of a document in under 3 minutes.

---

### **Scene 1: The Corporate Dashboard (30 sec)**
*   **Action:** Login as `pranali@northstar-tech.com`.
*   **Talking Point:** "Welcome to Northstar Sign. Here on the dashboard, you see the active pulse of our mid-sized technology firm, with 12 months of organic signing history across HR, Finance, and Legal."
*   **Show:** Point out the status distribution chart and the recent activity list.

### **Scene 2: Document Creation & Upload (30 sec)**
*   **Action:** Click 'New Document'. Title it "Employment Agreement".
*   **Action:** Upload the `test_upload.pdf`.
*   **Talking Point:** "I'll start by creating a new HR workflow. The system securely handles the PDF upload and initializes our draft state."

### **Scene 3: Visual Editor (45 sec)**
*   **Action:** Add a signer (e.g., `michael@northstar-tech.com`).
*   **Action:** Select the 'Signature' tool and place a box near the bottom.
*   **Action:** Switch signers and place a 'Date' field.
*   **Talking Point:** "Our visual editor uses a percentage-based coordinate system. This means whether Michael opens this on an iPhone or a 4K monitor, the signature will be placed with pixel-perfect accuracy."

### **Scene 4: Activation & Signing (45 sec)**
*   **Action:** Click 'Finish & Review' -> 'Activate'.
*   **Action:** (Simulate Michael) Open the welcome tunnel for the link.
*   **Action:** Sign the document using a cursive font.
*   **Talking Point:** "Once activated, Michael receives a secure, single-use token. The signing tunnel is mobile-optimized and ensures legal disclosure before the signature is captured."

### **Scene 5: Completion & Audit (30 sec)**
*   **Action:** Return to the Manager dashboard.
*   **Action:** Show the document status is now 'COMPLETED'.
*   **Action:** Open the document details and show the **Audit Trail**.
*   **Talking Point:** "The document is now finalized. Every step—from creation to the final PDF generation—is recorded in our immutable audit ledger for compliance."

---

### **Pro-Tips for Recording:**
1.  **Close unnecessary tabs:** Make the browser window clean.
2.  **Zoom in slightly:** Set browser zoom to 110% so the UI elements are clear on video.
3.  **No Mouse Jiggles:** Move your mouse with purpose. Slow, deliberate movements look more professional.
